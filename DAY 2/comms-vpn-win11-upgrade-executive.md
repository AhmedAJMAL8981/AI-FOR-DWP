# VPN / Windows 11 Upgrade — Executive Summary

**Ref:** T-1008 (VPN connects, no internal access after Win11 upgrade)

**Executive:**
Your access and data were never at risk. A recent Windows 11 upgrade caused a small number of laptops to lose their VPN setup, so some staff could connect to VPN but not reach internal systems. Our team found the cause, restored the correct VPN configuration, and confirmed full access to all internal systems again. No files or data were lost. No action is needed from you.
