# VPN / Windows 11 Upgrade — Team Message

**Ref:** T-1008 (VPN connects, no internal access after Win11 upgrade)

**Team:**
Hi team — after the recent Windows 11 upgrade, some laptops lost their VPN setup, so those devices could connect to VPN but couldn't reach internal files, drives, or systems. This has now been fixed: the VPN client was reinstalled and configured correctly, and access to all internal systems has been confirmed working again, with no data lost. If you still see this issue — VPN connects but you can't reach internal apps or shared drives — please restart your laptop first. If the problem continues, contact the Service Desk and reference this VPN/Windows 11 issue.
