# Stakeholder Update — Floor 3 Finance Login Issue
**Date:** 2024-03-15  
**Time of update:** Pending confirmation  
**Audience:** Non-technical stakeholders  

---

## What happened?

This morning, between approximately 07:40 and 07:55, a number of computers used by Finance staff on Floor 3 were unable to show the normal login screen. Affected users saw a blank or failed login experience and could not access their machines.

The issue affected most machines on Floor 3 in the Finance area. A small number of machines on the same floor were unaffected and are working normally.

---

## What caused it?

Overnight, a planned network migration changed some of the address settings used to connect machines to our internal systems. The automatic settings that floor devices receive when they start up were not updated as part of that migration, so affected machines started up this morning unable to find the internal systems they need to log in correctly.

This has now been identified as the cause.

---

## What is being done?

The IT infrastructure team has identified exactly what needs to be corrected and is updating the affected network settings. Machines that were manually updated ahead of the migration are already working normally, which confirms the fix is straightforward.

---

## When will it be resolved?

A resolution time is currently being confirmed. A further update will be provided once a completion time is known or the fix has been applied.

---

## Do users need to do anything?

**No action is required from users at this time.**

Once the fix has been applied, affected machines may need to be restarted. Users will be notified if any action is needed from them.
