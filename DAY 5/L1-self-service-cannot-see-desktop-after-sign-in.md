# I Signed In But Only See a Black Screen

If you sign in and only see a black screen, do not worry. Your files and work data are safe.

## What You Can Do Yourself
1. Wait 30 seconds.
Sometimes the screen appears after a short delay.

2. Log out and log back in.
If you can open your profile menu, choose Log out, then log back in.

3. If the screen is still black, close the work program fully and open it again.
Then log in one more time.

4. If it still does not work, restart your computer once.
After restart, open the work program and log in again.

5. If needed, use another work option while waiting.
For example, use email in your browser or your approved phone work apps so you can continue urgent work.

## Important Reassurance
- This issue does not delete your files.
- Your documents, mailbox, and saved work remain in your account.

## Contact the Service Desk If...
Contact the service desk if the black screen is still there after one restart and one more login try, or if this keeps happening on the same day.

## Include These Details in Your Ticket
- Your work login name
- The time the problem first happened
- Whether waiting 30 seconds helped or not
- Whether logging out and back in helped or not
- Whether restarting helped or not
- A picture of the black screen (if possible)
- Your computer name (if known)
- Any message shown on screen (exact words)
