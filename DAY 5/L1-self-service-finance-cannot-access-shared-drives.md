# Finance Team Cannot Open Company Folders or Files

Version: v1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

## Overview
If you cannot open Finance team folders or files, do not panic. This is usually a temporary problem opening work files and can often be fixed with a few quick steps.

## What You Can Try
1. Wait 2 minutes, then try opening the folder or file again.
2. Check your internet by opening any common website in your internet app.
3. If you are working from home, disconnect and reconnect your work connection app, then try again.
4. Close the folder window (or the app where the message appears), open it again, and try again.
5. Sign out of your computer, sign in again, and try opening the folder.
6. Restart your computer once, sign in, and try again.
7. If the problem started right after sign-in, wait 5 minutes after sign-in before trying again.

## What Information to Collect
Include these details when you ask for help:
- Your full work email address
- Your computer name (if known)
- The exact time the problem started
- Whether the problem happens for all Finance folders or only one folder
- The exact message shown on screen (or a screen photo)
- Your location (office or home)
- Whether each step above helped or not

## Contact the Service Desk If...
- You still cannot open Finance folders after completing all steps above.
- The problem keeps returning on the same day.
- Multiple teammates report the same problem at the same time.
- You see a new or unusual message on screen.

## Reassurance Message
Your files and data are safe. This problem only affects opening files, not saving files. Your documents are not deleted, and the support team can help restore access quickly.
