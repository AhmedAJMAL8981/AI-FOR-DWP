## Top 3 Themes to Act on Today (Ranked by Impact + Volume)

### 1) AVD sign-in and account lockouts (Count: 7, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks here: Highest-impact category (users unable to work at all) and the largest blocker volume, including repeated lockouts and failed sign-ins tied to urgent work.

One sentence to manager: "Our top priority is AVD sign-in/account lockout failures because they are both the most frequent blocker and a complete work stoppage for affected users."

### 2) OneDrive missing files and sync concerns (Count: 4, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks here: Even with lower count than some friction issues, this is a hard blocker involving apparent data unavailability and deadline risk (meeting/client report references).

One sentence to manager: "OneDrive missing-file incidents are the second priority because they create immediate business risk by blocking access to critical documents before deadlines."

### 3) Shared drive access failures (S: / Finance drive) (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)
Why this ranks here: Although fewer reports, this remains a full blocker for core Finance workflows (for example month-end reporting) and therefore outranks higher-volume minor/friction themes.

One sentence to manager: "Shared drive access failures rank third because they directly stop Finance delivery, so we should treat them ahead of higher-volume but non-blocking usability issues."

## Ranking Principle Applied

This ranking intentionally prioritizes blocker severity over raw count: a lower-volume blocker is ranked ahead of higher-volume friction or minor themes when it prevents users from working.
