# All-Staff Notice: AVD Sign-In and Account Lockouts

We are aware of increased reports this morning of AVD sign-in failures and account lockouts. Our support teams are actively investigating and working this as a priority.

As an interim step, please try the following:
1. Close all remote desktop/AVD windows, then sign out of your work account on the AVD sign-in page and sign in again.
2. If you recently changed your password, lock your laptop (Windows + L), unlock it with your new password, then try AVD one more time.

If you still cannot sign in after 2 tries, please contact the Service Desk and share your username, the exact error message, the time it happened, and whether this is your first or repeated lockout this week.

Thank you for your patience. We know this is disruptive and we are treating it urgently. We will share the next status update by 11:30 AM today, even if the issue is still in progress.
