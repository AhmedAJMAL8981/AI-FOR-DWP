# Self-Service Answers

## AVD sign-in and account lockouts
1. Close all remote desktop/AVD windows, then sign out of your work account on the AVD sign-in page and sign in again.
2. If you recently changed your password, lock your laptop (Windows + L), unlock it with the new password, then try AVD one more time.

If you still cannot sign in after 2 tries or see an account locked message, contact the Service Desk and share: your username, exact error message, time it happened, and whether this is your first or repeated lockout this week.

## OneDrive missing files and sync concerns
1. Open OneDrive on the web (office.com), check if the files are there, and search by file name.
2. In File Explorer, open your OneDrive folder and click the OneDrive cloud icon near the clock to resume sync if it is paused.

If files are still missing or you cannot open key documents, contact the Service Desk and share: 2-3 missing file names, folder path, last time you saw them, and any error message shown.

## Shared drive access failures (S: / Finance drive)
1. Disconnect and reconnect your VPN, then wait 1 minute and try opening the S: drive again.
2. In File Explorer, type \\your-company-server\finance (or your usual Finance path) to check whether the folder opens directly.

If access is still denied, contact the Service Desk and share: your username, exact denied message, the drive/path you tried, time of failure, and whether teammates can access it.
