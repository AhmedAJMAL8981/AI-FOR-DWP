# FinBridge IT — User Notices: Known Issues After Platform Update

**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## Notice 1: Trouble accessing the shared credentials vault?

We are aware that some users cannot open the credentials vault following the recent update. You are not alone and we are working on a fix.

**Try this first:**
1. Sign out completely, wait 30 seconds, then sign back in and try again.
2. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl + Shift + Delete → clear all → restart browser) and retry.

If it still does not open, please contact the Service Desk straight away. To help us fix this quickly, tell us:
- Your name and team
- The exact error message or what you see on screen
- How many days this has been happening

---

## Notice 2: Locked out of the admin console?

Some users are finding they cannot get into the admin console after the recent update. We are investigating as a priority.

**Try this first:**
1. Wait two minutes, then try signing in again — a brief delay can clear a temporary block.
2. Try a different browser or an incognito/private window.

If you are still locked out, contact the Service Desk. To speed up your request, please share:
- Your name and the account you use to sign in
- Whether colleagues on your team are seeing the same thing
- The time you first noticed the lockout
