# L1 Self-Service KB: Unexpected Copilot Content (Floor 6)
**v 1.0, 07/08/2026, status : Draft**

If Copilot shows content you do not expect, stay calm. Your data is safe, and our team is actively checking access settings.

## What you can do now
1. Stop using Copilot for now.
2. Do not share, copy, or forward the content you saw.
3. Take a screenshot of what appeared (if possible).
4. Sign out of your work apps, then sign back in once.
5. Wait 5 minutes and try again.

## What to include in your ticket
- Your name and team (Floor 6 Legal)
- Time you saw the issue
- What you searched or asked (exact words if possible)
- A short description of what appeared
- Screenshot (if available)
- Device name (shown on your laptop sticker or Settings)
- Whether the issue happened once or more than once

We are treating this as a priority and reviewing it with security and legal teams.

**Contact the Service Desk immediately if you still see unexpected content, or if you are unsure whether to continue working.**
