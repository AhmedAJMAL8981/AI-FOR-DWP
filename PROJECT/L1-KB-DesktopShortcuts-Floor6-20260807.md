# L1 Self-Service KB: Missing Desktop Shortcuts (Floor 6)
**v 1.0, 07/08/2026, status : Draft**

If your desktop shortcuts are missing, do not worry. Your files and data are safe. This issue can happen after system changes and is being checked by IT.

## What you can do now
1. Sign out of your computer.
2. Wait 1 minute.
3. Sign back in.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes for your desktop to fully load.
5. Check if your shortcuts are back.

## What to include in your ticket
- Your full name
- Team and location (Floor 6 Legal)
- Device name (from laptop sticker or Settings)
- Time you first noticed the issue
- Names of missing shortcuts
- Whether this happened once or every sign-in
- Screenshot of your desktop (if possible)

Thank you for your patience. We know this is frustrating, and we are working to restore normal desktop views safely.

**Contact the Service Desk if your shortcuts are still missing after one sign-out/sign-in, or if you cannot open key work apps.**
