# L1 Self-Service KB: Slow Login or Cannot Sign In (Floor 6)
**v 1.0, 07/08/2026, status : Draft**

If your sign-in is slow or fails, please don’t worry. Your files and data are safe. Our support team is actively checking this issue.

## What you can do now
1. Sign out of your computer.
2. Wait 1 minute.
3. Sign back in once.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes for your desktop to load.
5. If still stuck, restart your computer once.
6. Try signing in again.

## Details to include in your ticket
- Your full name
- Team and location (Floor 6 Legal)
- Device name (from laptop sticker or Settings)
- Time the issue started
- What happened: slow sign-in, error message, or both
- Exact error message (if shown)
- Whether this happened once or every sign-in
- Screenshot of the screen (if possible)

Thank you for your patience. We understand this is disruptive and we are working carefully to restore normal access.

**Contact the Service Desk if you still cannot sign in after one restart, or if sign-in keeps taking more than 5 minutes.**
